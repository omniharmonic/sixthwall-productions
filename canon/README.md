# Canon

The source texts behind the site.

The site has two layers, and they are the first two walls. The pages in
`src/content/` are Wall III — the staging: what is said in public, lit and
held for a visitor. The texts in this directory are Wall II — the word: what
is actually meant, written down so it can be carried by people who were not
in the room when it was worked out. The pages are derived from the canon.
When they disagree, the canon is wrong or the page is; fix one.

## Where it comes from

Almost everything here is drawn from one recorded conversation between the two
founders on 19 August 2026, transcribed as *Cultural Wellness Engagement
Strategies.md* (the transcriber's title, not ours). Citations of the form
`[T:123]` are line numbers in that transcript. Where a text also draws on the
Herd of God production bible or Duncan's theological statement, it says so.

Three kinds of content appear, and the texts distinguish them:

- **Transcribed.** Said in the conversation, tidied for the page. Cited.
- **Synthesised.** Two or more things said in the conversation, joined.
  Cited to each.
- **`[PROPOSED]`.** Mine — a connection, a name, or a piece of doctrine that
  the conversation reaches toward but does not state. Marked so that the
  founders can accept it, alter it, or throw it out. Nothing marked
  `[PROPOSED]` should be treated as settled.

## What is not here

This repository is public. Nothing in the canon names a third party who was
discussed in the conversation, describes anyone's private life, or carries
material the Herd of God holds sealed (the Calf's own wish; the eighth
angel's name; what the god asks for on waking). Those exist elsewhere and
belong elsewhere. The rule is simple: if it could not be read aloud at the
foot of the stage, it is not canon.

## Conventions

Write in the company's voice, not either founder's. The company's voice
explains mechanism rather than meaning, owns its positions in the first person
plural, lands long sentences on short flat ones, and does not hedge. It may be
funny. It may not be cute.

Keep the fidelity. A sentence that sounds right but was not said goes under
`[PROPOSED]` or it goes nowhere.
