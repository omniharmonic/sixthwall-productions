The fourth wall has been broken so often it is furniture. A wink to camera does not close the gap between player and consumer. It consummates it.

So we build past it — for the wall at the exit, where people carry a world home and begin living inside it. This is not a metaphor. It is a craft, and it can be practised on purpose.
