Who is it for, how are they drawn, what are they asked to give. Not a ticket: attention, and something beneath attention we do not have a polite word for, offered on trust, to receive a circulation back.

The gap between the stage and the seats is the oldest gap there is. Every theatre stands on it. Most never touch it.
