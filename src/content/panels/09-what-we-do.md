Every wall is coming down at once — the church's, the state's, the screen's — and a people who have found out their stories are made must learn to make them on purpose, together, in the open. That is rehearsed somewhere, or it is never learned.

We rehearse it here, for the wall at the exit, where a play becomes the way some people live. Not a metaphor. A craft.
